Fluid Mechanics — Core Formulas

Condensed reference for hydrostatics, Bernoulli equation, and pipe flow dynamics.

Hydrostatic Pressure

Pressure variation in incompressible static fluid:

P = P₀ + ρ·g·h

Continuity Equation

Conservation of mass for incompressible flow:

A₁ · V₁ = A₂ · V₂ = Q

Bernoulli Equation

Along a streamline without friction or work:

P/γ + V²/(2g) + z = Constant

Reynolds Number (Re)

Ratio of inertial to viscous forces:

Re = (ρ · V · D) / μ = (V · D) / ν

Laminar: Re < 2300 | Turbulent: Re > 4000
