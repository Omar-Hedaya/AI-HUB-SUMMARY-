Key practice questions and recurring patterns from past examinations.

Question 1: Steam Power Cycle Analysis

A simple Rankine cycle uses water as working fluid. Superheated vapor enters the turbine at 3 MPa, 350 °C and condenser pressure is 10 kPa. If the pump operates isentropically, find the cycle thermal efficiency.

Exam Tip: Always look up h₁ and s₁ from superheated steam tables, then find quality x₂ at the turbine exit using s₂ = s₁.
Question 2: Otto Cycle Compression Ratio

An ideal Otto cycle has a compression ratio r = 8. At the beginning of compression, air is at 100 kPa and 300 K. The heat addition is 800 kJ/kg. Compute the maximum temperature and net work output per cycle.

Exam Tip: Recall η_th = 1 - (1 / r^(k-1)) where k = 1.4 for air at room temperature.
