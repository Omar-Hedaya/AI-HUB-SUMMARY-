Introduction to Thermodynamics & Energy Concepts

Engineering Thermodynamics — Fundamentals of Systems, Boundaries, and the First Law

1. Fundamental Definitions

Thermodynamics is the science of energy, heat, work, and the transformations between them. In engineering, we analyze systems defined by boundaries across which energy and matter may or may not flow.

System Classifications

  • Closed System (Control Mass): No mass enters or leaves the boundary. Energy (heat and work) can cross.
  • Open System (Control Volume): Both mass and energy cross the control surface.
  • Isolated System: Neither mass nor energy can cross the boundaries.

2. The First Law of Thermodynamics

Energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it can only change from one form to another. For a stationary closed system undergoing a change of state:

ΔE = ΔU + ΔKE + ΔPE = Q - W

Where:

  • ΔU: Change in internal energy (kJ)
  • Q: Net heat added to the system (kJ)
  • W: Net work done by the system (kJ)
