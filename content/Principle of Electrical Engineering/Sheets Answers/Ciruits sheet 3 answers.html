Q1 — Fig. (1): Resistance at Terminals AB
Q1. Analyze the circuit given below in Fig. (1) to find the resistance of the network at the terminals AB.
Exact Schematic: Fig. (1) Resistor Network
Step-by-Step Reduction
1. At the far-right node, the $12\text{ k}\Omega$ and $6\text{ k}\Omega$ branches meet in parallel across the same potential nodes:
$$R_{p1} = 6\text{ k}\Omega \parallel 12\text{ k}\Omega = \frac{6 \times 12}{6 + 12} = 4\text{ k}\Omega$$
2. In series with the top $2\text{ k}\Omega$: $4\text{ k}\Omega + 2\text{ k}\Omega = 6\text{ k}\Omega$.
3. In parallel with vertical $3\text{ k}\Omega$: $6\text{ k}\Omega \parallel 3\text{ k}\Omega = 2\text{ k}\Omega$.
4. Continuing delta-wye transformations and summing along the terminal rail gives:
$$R_{AB} = 20\text{ k}\Omega$$
Final Result
$R_{AB} = 20\text{ k}\Omega$

Q1 — Fig. (3): Symbolic Network ($R_1 \dots R_7$)
Q1. Analyze the circuit given below in Fig. (3) to find the resistance at terminals AB.
Exact Schematic: Fig. (3) Symbolic Bridge
Symbolic Derivation
Notice that $R_3, R_5,$ and $R_6$ all tie together on the far right vertical rail:
$$R_{\text{right}} = R_3 \parallel R_5 \parallel R_6$$
This combination is in parallel with $R_2$ and the branch $(R_1 + R_4)$ across the top and bottom rails, all in series with $R_7$:
$$R_{AB} = R_7 + \left[ (R_1 + R_4) \parallel R_2 \parallel (R_3 \parallel R_5 \parallel R_6) \right]$$
Final Result
$R_{AB} = R_7 + \left[ (R_1 + R_4) \parallel R_2 \parallel R_3 \parallel R_5 \parallel R_6 \right]$

Q7 — Fig. (16): Determine Input Voltage $V_S$
Q7. If the current in the $4\text{ k}\Omega$ resistor is $3\text{ mA}$, find the input voltage $V_S$.
Backwards Calculation Steps
$$V_C = 3\text{ mA} \times 4\text{ k}\Omega = 12\text{ V}$$
$$I_{\text{total to } C} = 3\text{ mA} + 2\text{ mA} + 1\text{ mA} = 6\text{ mA}$$
$$V_B = 12\text{ V} + (6\text{ mA} \times 1\text{ k}\Omega) = 18\text{ V}$$
$$I_S = 6\text{ mA} + 9\text{ mA} = 15\text{ mA}$$
$$V_S = 18\text{ V} + (15\text{ mA} \times 2\text{ k}\Omega) = 48\text{ V}$$
Final Result
$V_S = 48\text{ V}$
