Part 1: Thermodynamics, Energy & Units — Complete Summary & Laws

Prepared strictly according to Dr. Ashraf Elsayed's syllabus (Faculty of Engineering at Shoubra, Benha University): Fundamentals of Energy, Conservation, Dimensions, and SI Conversion Standards.

Part 1: Introduction, Energy & Unit Systems

👈 اسحب الجدول أفقياً لتصفح كامل القوانين والوصف 👉
Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters
Thermodynamics DefinitionGreek: therme (Heat) + dynamis (Power)Science dealing with energy conversion from one form to another and substance properties.
Total Energy ($E$)$E = U + KE + PE$Sum of macroscopic ($KE = \frac{1}{2}mV^2$, $PE = mgz$) and microscopic internal energy ($U$).
Conservation of Energy (1st Law)$E_{in} - E_{out} = \Delta E_{system}$Energy cannot be created or destroyed; it merely changes from one form to another.
Force Secondary Law$F = m \cdot a = m \cdot g$$1\text{ N} = 1\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}^2$. In English system, $\text{lb}_f$ is base unit ($1\text{ lb}_f = 4.448\text{ N}$).
Work / Energy Unit$1\text{ J} = 1\text{ N}\cdot\text{m} = 1\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{s}^2$$1\text{ MJ} = 10^3\text{ kJ} = 10^6\text{ J}$. In English: $1\text{ Btu} = 1055\text{ J}$.
Power Unit (Watt)$\dot{W} = \frac{W}{\Delta t} \implies 1\text{ W} = 1\text{ J/s}$Rate of energy transfer per unit time ($1\text{ MW} = 10^3\text{ kW} = 10^6\text{ W}$).

📌 Essential Key Rules for Part 1

  • Primary Dimensions: Mass ($m$), Length ($L$), Time ($t$), and Temperature ($T$) are base units in SI.
  • Dimensional Homogeneity: Every additive term in any equation must possess strictly identical physical units.

Part 2: Thermodynamic Systems & Boundaries

👈 اسحب الجدول أفقياً لتصفح كامل القوانين والوصف 👉
Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters
Closed System (Control Mass)$\Delta m = 0 \quad (m = \text{const})$No mass can cross boundary. Energy (heat $Q$ and work $W$) can cross boundary freely.
Open System (Control Volume)$\Delta m \ne 0 \text{ and } \Delta E \ne 0$Selected volume in space where both mass and energy cross the control surface.

Part 3: Properties of a System, Density & Specifics

👈 اسحب الجدول أفقياً لتصفح كامل القوانين والوصف 👉
Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters
Intensive PropertiesIndependent of mass ($m^0$)Temperature ($T$), Pressure ($P$), Density ($\rho$).
Extensive PropertiesDependent on mass / size ($m^1$)Total Mass ($m$), Total Volume ($V$), Total Energy ($E$).
Density ($\rho$)$\rho = \frac{m}{V} \quad [\text{kg/m}^3]$Mass per unit volume. For water at $4^\circ\text{C}$: $\rho_{H_2O} = 1000\text{ kg/m}^3$.

Lecture 1 Solved Examples

Problem 1: Liquid Properties Calculation
Volume $V = 6\text{ m}^3$, Weight $W = 44\text{ kN}$. Calculate specific weight ($w$), mass density ($\rho$), specific volume ($v$), and specific gravity ($S$).
الخطوة 1: حساب الوزن النوعي (Specific Weight $w$)
القانون: $$w = \frac{W}{V}$$
التعويض: $$w = \frac{44\text{ kN}}{6\text{ m}^3} = 7.333\text{ kN/m}^3$$
النتيجة: $w = 7.333\text{ kN/m}^3 = 7333\text{ N/m}^3$
الخطوة 2: حساب الكثافة الكتلية (Density $\rho$)
القانون: $$\rho = \frac{w}{g}$$
التعويض: $$\rho = \frac{7333.33\text{ N/m}^3}{9.81\text{ m/s}^2} = 747.54\text{ kg/m}^3$$
النتيجة: $\rho = 747.5\text{ kg/m}^3$
الخطوة 3: حساب الحجم النوعي (Specific Volume $v$)
القانون: $$v = \frac{1}{\rho}$$
التعويض: $$v = \frac{1}{747.54\text{ kg/m}^3} = 0.001338\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$$
النتيجة: $v = 0.00134\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$
الخطوة 4: حساب الوزن النوعي النسبي (Specific Gravity $S$)
القانون: $$S = \frac{\rho}{\rho_{water}}$$
التعويض: $$S = \frac{747.54}{1000} = 0.747$$
النتيجة: $SG = 0.747$
Problem 2: Petrol Weight Calculation
Volume $V = 1\text{ liter} = 0.001\text{ m}^3$, Specific gravity $SG = 0.7$. Calculate $\rho$, $w$, and total weight $W$.
الخطوة 1: حساب الكثافة ($\rho$)
القانون: $$\rho = SG \cdot \rho_{water}$$
التعويض: $$\rho = 0.7 \times 1000 = 700\text{ kg/m}^3$$
النتيجة: $\rho = 700\text{ kg/m}^3$
الخطوة 2: حساب الوزن النوعي ($w$)
القانون: $$w = \rho \cdot g$$
التعويض: $$w = 700 \times 9.81 = 6867\text{ N/m}^3$$
النتيجة: $w = 6867\text{ N/m}^3$
الخطوة 3: حساب الوزن الإجمالي ($W$)
القانون: $$W = w \cdot V$$
التعويض: $$W = 6867\text{ N/m}^3 \times 0.001\text{ m}^3 = 6.867\text{ N}$$
النتيجة: $W = 6.867\text{ N}$

Part 2: Ideal Gas Equation of State

👈 اسحب الجدول أفقياً لتصفح كامل القوانين والوصف 👉
Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters
Ideal Gas Law$PV = mRT$$P$ in $\text{Pa}$, $V$ in $\text{m}^3$, $m$ in $\text{kg}$, $T$ in $\text{K}$, $R$ in $\text{J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$.
Specific Volume Form$Pv = RT$$v = \frac{V}{m} \quad [\text{m}^3/\text{kg}]$ is specific volume.
Density Form$P = \rho RT$$\rho = \frac{1}{v} \quad [\text{kg/m}^3]$ is density.
Gas Constant ($R$)$R = \frac{\bar{R}}{M} = \frac{R_u}{M}$Universal gas constant $\bar{R} = 8.314\text{ kJ/(kmol}\cdot\text{K)}$; $M$ is molar mass.
Change of State$\frac{P_1 V_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2 V_2}{T_2}$Applies for a fixed mass of ideal gas between state 1 and state 2.

Part 3: Specific Heats, Joule's Law & Enthalpy

👈 اسحب الجدول أفقياً لتصفح كامل القوانين والوصف 👉
Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters
$C_v$ (Constant Volume)$C_v = \frac{R}{\gamma - 1}$Energy required to raise unit mass temperature by $1^\circ\text{C}$ at constant volume.
$C_p$ (Constant Pressure)$C_p = \frac{\gamma R}{\gamma - 1}$Energy required to raise unit mass temperature by $1^\circ\text{C}$ at constant pressure.
Mayer's Relation$C_p = C_v + R$Connects specific heats and gas constant for ideal gases.
Specific Heat Ratio$\gamma = \frac{C_p}{C_v}$$\gamma = 1.4$ for air and diatomic gases; $\gamma = 1.667$ for monatomic gases.
Joule's Law ($\Delta U$)$\Delta U = m C_v (T_2 - T_1)$Internal energy of an ideal gas depends only on temperature.
Enthalpy ($\Delta H$)$\Delta H = m C_p (T_2 - T_1)$Enthalpy of an ideal gas depends only on temperature.

Part 4: Real Gas Model & Compressibility Factor ($Z$)

👈 اسحب الجدول أفقياً لتصفح كامل القوانين والوصف 👉
Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters
Real Gas DefinitionFinite volume & intermolecular forcesSignificant deviation from ideal gas behavior at high pressure or low temperature.
Compressibility Factor ($Z$)$Z = \frac{P v}{R T} = \frac{V_{actual}}{V_{ideal}}$$Z = 1$ for ideal gases; $Z \ne 1$ for real gases.
Real Gas Equation$P V = Z m R T \implies m = \frac{PV}{ZRT}$State equation incorporating compressibility correction factor $Z$.
Percentage Error$\text{Error \%} = \frac{|m_{actual} - m_{ideal}|}{m_{actual}} \times 100$Quantifies deviation caused by assuming ideal gas behavior.

Part 6: Mechanical Force Balances on Pistons

Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters

Lecture 2 Solved Examples (Complete 11 Worked Problems)

Example 1: Piston Force Balance
$m = 20\text{ kg}, P_{atm} = 0.1\text{ MPa}, A = 10\text{ cm}^2 = 0.001\text{ m}^2$. Find $P_{abs}$ and $P_{gage}$.
الخطوة 1: قانون اتزان القوى على المكبس
القانون: $$P_{abs} \cdot A = P_{atm} \cdot A + m \cdot g \implies P_{abs} = P_{atm} + \frac{m \cdot g}{A}$$
التعويض: $$P_{abs} = 100\text{ kPa} + \frac{20 \times 9.81}{0.001 \times 10^3}\text{ kPa} = 100 + 196.2 = 296.2\text{ kPa}$$
النتيجة: $P_{abs} = 296.2\text{ kPa} = 0.2962\text{ MPa}$
الخطوة 2: حساب الضغط المقاس ($P_{gage}$)
القانون: $$P_{gage} = \frac{m \cdot g}{A} = \frac{20 \times 9.81}{0.001 \times 10^3} = 196.2\text{ kPa}$$
النتيجة: $P_{gage} = 196.2\text{ kPa}$
Example 2: Piston with Compressed Spring
$m_p = 3.2\text{ kg}, A = 35\text{ cm}^2 = 35 \times 10^{-4}\text{ m}^2, F_{spring} = 150\text{ N}, P_{atm} = 95\text{ kPa}$. Find internal pressure $P$.
خطوات الحل:
القانون: $$P = P_{atm} + \frac{m_p \cdot g + F_{spring}}{A}$$
التعويض: $$P = 95\text{ kPa} + \frac{(3.2 \times 9.81) + 150}{35 \times 10^{-4} \times 1000}\text{ kPa} = 95 + \frac{181.39}{3.5} = 95 + 51.83 = 146.8\text{ kPa}$$
النتيجة: $P = 147\text{ kPa}$

Part 1: Energy Transfer by Heat ($Q$)

👈 اسحب الجدول أفقياً لتصفح كامل القوانين والوصف 👉
Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters
Heat Definition ($Q$)Form of energy transferred via $\Delta T$Energy exchanged between two systems solely driven by temperature difference.
Heat Driving ForceDirection: Higher $T \longrightarrow$ Lower $T$Heat transfer ceases when thermal equilibrium is established ($T_1 = T_2 \implies Q = 0$).
Adiabatic Process$Q = 0 \quad (\text{No heat transfer})$Occurs if system is well insulated or both systems are at the identical temperature.
Specific Heat Transfer ($q$)$q = \frac{Q}{m} \quad [\text{kJ/kg}]$Amount of heat transferred per unit mass of the working substance.
Rate of Heat Transfer ($\dot{Q}$)$\dot{Q} = \frac{Q}{\Delta t} \quad [\text{kJ/s} \equiv \text{kW}]$Heat transfer per unit time (heat power).
Sign Convention for Heat$Q_{in} > 0 \ (+), \quad Q_{out} < 0 \ (-)$Heat added to the system is POSITIVE; heat released/rejected is NEGATIVE.

Part 2: Energy Transfer by Work ($W$)

👈 اسحب الجدول أفقياً لتصفح كامل القوانين والوصف 👉
Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters
Mechanical Work ($W$)$W = F \times \text{distance} \quad [\text{kJ}]$Energy transfer associated with a force acting through a distance.
Specific Work ($w$)$w = \frac{W}{m} \quad [\text{kJ/kg}]$Work performed per unit mass of the system.
Power ($\dot{W}$)$\dot{W} = \frac{W}{\Delta t} \quad [\text{kJ/s} \equiv \text{kW}]$Rate of doing work per unit time ($1\text{ MW} = 1000\text{ kW}$).
Sign Convention for Work$W_{out} > 0 \ (+), \quad W_{in} < 0 \ (-)$Work done BY the system (expansion) is POSITIVE; work done ON system (compression) is NEGATIVE.

Area on $P-V$ Diagram$\text{Area under curve} = W_b$The area under the process curve on a $P-V$ diagram equals the magnitude of moving boundary work.

Part 4: Polytropic & Isentropic Processes

Polytropic Process Law$P V^n = C \implies P_1 V_1^n = P_2 V_2^n$Models actual expansion and compression processes with polytropic index $n$ and friction.
Polytropic Work Formula$W_{1-2} = \frac{P_2 V_2 - P_1 V_1}{1 - n} = \frac{m R (T_2 - T_1)}{1 - n}$Moving boundary work for a closed system undergoing a polytropic process ($n \ne 1$).

Isentropic Work Formula$W_{1-2} = \frac{P_2 V_2 - P_1 V_1}{1 - \gamma} = \frac{m R (T_2 - T_1)}{1 - \gamma}$Work done during reversible adiabatic expansion/compression.

Concept / TopicFormula / EquationDescription & Key Parameters
Isothermal Process Law$T = C \implies P V = C \quad (n = 1)$Process occurring at strictly constant temperature. $P_1 V_1 = P_2 V_2$.
Isothermal Work Formula$W_{1-2} = P_1 V_1 \ln\left(\frac{V_2}{V_1}\right) = m R T \ln\left(\frac{P_1}{P_2}\right)$Boundary work for constant-temperature process ($V_2/V_1 = P_1/P_2$).
Isobaric Process Law$P = C \implies \frac{V_1}{T_1} = \frac{V_2}{T_2} \quad (n = 0)$Process occurring at strictly constant pressure (freely floating weighted piston).

Assignment Problem 1: Multi-Process Steam States
مسألة الواجب 1: Water vapor initially at $P_1 = 0.3\text{ MPa}$ and $T_1 = 300^\circ\text{C}$ (State 1) is contained within a piston-cylinder assembly. The water is cooled at a constant volume until its temperature reaches $T_2 = 200^\circ\text{C}$ (State 2). The water is then compressed at a constant temperature to a state where the pressure is $P_3 = 2.5\text{ MPa}$ (State 3).
المطلوب: (a) Locate states 1, 2, and 3 on the $T-v$ and $P-v$ diagrams. (b) Determine the specific volume at states 1, 2, and 3, and the quality $x_2$ at state 2.
الخطوة 1: تحديد الحالة الأولى (State 1: $P_1 = 0.3\text{ MPa}, T_1 = 300^\circ\text{C}$)
• من جدول الضغط (Table A-5) عند ضغط $P = 0.3\text{ MPa}$، نجد أن درجة حرارة التشبع: $T_{sat} = 133.52^\circ\text{C}$.
• بما أن درجة الحرارة المعطاة $T_1 = 300^\circ\text{C} > T_{sat}$، إذن الحالة بخار محمص (Superheated Vapor).
• من جدول البخار المحمص (Table A-6) عند $P = 0.3\text{ MPa}$ و $T = 300^\circ\text{C}$ نجد الحجم النوعي مباشرة:
$v_1 = 0.87535\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$
الخطوة 2: تحديد الحالة الثانية (State 2: تبريد بثبوت الحجم $v_2 = v_1$ إلى $T_2 = 200^\circ\text{C}$)
• العملية من 1 إلى 2 تمت بثبوت الحجم: $v_2 = v_1 = 0.87535\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$ عند درجة حرارة $T_2 = 200^\circ\text{C}$.
• نذهب لجدول درجات الحرارة (Table A-4) عند $T = 200^\circ\text{C}$ لنقارن الحجم بالقيم الحرجة:
- حجم السائل المشبع: $v_f = 0.001157\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$
- حجم البخار المشبع: $v_g = 0.12721\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$
• نلاحظ أن: $v_2 = 0.87535\text{ m}^3/\text{kg} > v_g = 0.12721\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$.
• الاستنتاج: الحالة 2 ما زالت في منطقة البخار المحمص (Superheated Vapor) ولم تدخل منطقة الخليط المشبع!
• الحجم النوعي: $v_2 = 0.87535\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$
• نسبة الجفاف ($x_2$): غير معرّفة (Not Applicable) لأن الحالة بخار محمص $100\%$
• الضغط $P_2$: من جدول A-6 عند $T = 200^\circ\text{C}$ بالاستكمال نجد $P_2 \approx 0.245\text{ MPa}$.
الخطوة 3: تحديد الحالة الثالثة (State 3: انضغاط بثبوت الحرارة $T_3 = 200^\circ\text{C}$ إلى $P_3 = 2.5\text{ MPa}$)
• عند درجة حرارة $T_3 = 200^\circ\text{C}$، ضغط التشبع المقابل هو: $P_{sat} = 1.554\text{ MPa}$.
• بما أن الضغط المعطى $P_3 = 2.5\text{ MPa} > P_{sat} = 1.554\text{ MPa}$، إذن تحول الماء إلى سائل مضغوط (Compressed / Subcooled Liquid).
• بما أن الضغط أقل من $5\text{ MPa}$ ولا توجد له جداول سائل مضغوط خاصة، نطبق تقريب السائل المشبع عند درجة الحرارة:
$$v_3 \approx v_f @ 200^\circ\text{C} = 0.001157\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$$
$v_3 = 0.001157\text{ m}^3/\text{kg}$
Assignment Problem 2: Vertical Cylinder with Stops
مسألة الواجب 2: Air is contained in a vertical cylinder fitted with a frictionless piston and a set of stops. The piston cross-sectional area is $A = 0.2\text{ m}^2$, and the air inside is initially at $P_1 = 200\text{ kPa}$ and $T_1 = 500^\circ\text{C} = 773.15\text{ K}$. Initial height $h_1 = 2\text{ m}$ (initial volume $V_1 = A \cdot h_1 = 0.4\text{ m}^3$). The air is cooled as a result of heat transfer to surroundings.
المطلوب:
(a) What is the temperature of the air inside the cylinder when the piston reaches the stops at height $h_{stops} = 1\text{ m}$?
(b) The cooling is now continued until the temperature reaches $T_3 = 20^\circ\text{C} = 293.15\text{ K}$, what is the pressure at this final state?
الخطوة 1: المعطيات وتحديد مسار العمليات
• مساحة مقطع المكبس: $A = 0.2\text{ m}^2$
• الحالة الابتدائية 1: $P_1 = 200\text{ kPa}, \quad T_1 = 500 + 273.15 = 773.15\text{ K}, \quad V_1 = 0.2 \times 2 = 0.4\text{ m}^3$
• المرحلة الأولى (1 إلى 2): المكبس يتحرك بحرية لأسفل، إذن الضغط ثابت (Isobaric Cooling): $P_2 = P_1 = 200\text{ kPa}$ حتى يستقر على الصدادات (Stops) عند ارتفاع $h_2 = 1\text{ m} \implies V_2 = 0.2 \times 1 = 0.2\text{ m}^3$.
• المرحلة الثانية (2 إلى 3): المكبس مثبت على الصدادات لا يمكنه النزول، إذن الحجم ثابت (Isochoric Cooling): $V_3 = V_2 = 0.2\text{ m}^3$ حتى تبرد درجة الحرارة إلى $T_3 = 20 + 273.15 = 293.15\text{ K}$.
المطلوب (a): درجة الحرارة عندما يلامس المكبس الصدادات ($T_2$)
• طالما أن المكبس يتحرك بحرية، فإن العملية تتم بثبوت الضغط ($P = \text{const}$):
$$\frac{V_1}{T_1} = \frac{V_2}{T_2}$$
• نعزل $T_2$ ونعوض بالقيم:
$$T_2 = T_1 \cdot \left(\frac{V_2}{V_1}\right) = 773.15 \cdot \left(\frac{0.2}{0.4}\right) = 773.15 \cdot 0.5 = 386.58\text{ K}$$
• بالدرجات المئوية: $T_2 = 386.58 - 273.15 = 113.43^\circ\text{C}$
$T_{stops} = 386.6\text{ K} \quad (113.4^\circ\text{C})$
المطلوب (b): الضغط النهائي عند تبريد الهواء إلى $20^\circ\text{C}$ ($P_3$)
• بعد استقرار المكبس على الصدادات، يصبح الحجم ثابتاً تماماً ($V = \text{const}$):
$$\frac{P_2}{T_2} = \frac{P_3}{T_3}$$
• نعزل $P_3$ ونعوض بالقيم المطلقة:
$$P_3 = P_2 \cdot \left(\frac{T_3}{T_2}\right) = 200\text{ kPa} \cdot \left(\frac{293.15\text{ K}}{386.58\text{ K}}\right)$$
$$P_3 = 200 \times 0.7583 = 151.66\text{ kPa}$$
$P_{final} = 151.7\text{ kPa}$
