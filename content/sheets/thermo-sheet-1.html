Thermodynamics — Problem Sheet 1

Topic: Closed Systems, Boundary Work, Polytropic Processes

Problem 1.1: Polytropic Expansion of an Ideal Gas 15 Points

A piston-cylinder device initially contains 0.05 m³ of nitrogen gas at 100 kPa and 27 °C. The gas is compressed polytropically according to the relation PV^1.3 = C until the volume is reduced to 0.01 m³. Calculate:

  • a) The final pressure of nitrogen in kPa
  • b) The boundary work done during this compression process in kJ

[Step 1] Final Pressure Calculation:

P₁ · V₁^n = P₂ · V₂^n

P₂ = P₁ · (V₁ / V₂)^n = 100 kPa · (0.05 / 0.01)^1.3 = 100 · (5)^1.3 = 810.3 kPa


[Step 2] Boundary Work:

W_b = (P₂V₂ - P₁V₁) / (1 - n) = [(810.3 · 0.01) - (100 · 0.05)] / (1 - 1.3)

W_b = (8.103 - 5.0) / (-0.3) = 3.103 / (-0.3) = -10.34 kJ (Work done ON system)

Problem 1.2: Rigid Tank Heating 10 Points

A rigid container with a volume of 0.8 m³ holds air at 200 kPa and 300 K. Heat is added until the pressure rises to 500 kPa. Assuming ideal gas behavior with Cv = 0.718 kJ/(kg·K) and R = 0.287 kJ/(kg·K), determine the mass of air and total heat transferred.

Mass of air: m = P₁V₁ / (R · T₁) = (200 · 0.8) / (0.287 · 300) = 1.858 kg

Final Temperature: T₂ = T₁ · (P₂ / P₁) = 300 · (500 / 200) = 750 K

Heat Transferred: Q = m · Cv · (T₂ - T₁) = 1.858 · 0.718 · (750 - 300) = +600.3 kJ
